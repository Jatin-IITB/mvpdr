KEY FINDINGS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Full Training Performance: 72.36% accuracy
   - Best achievable with complete training data

2. Extreme Few-Shot (1-shot): 34.55% accuracy
   - 47.7% of full performance with just 1 example per class
   - Better than random (27 classes = 3.7% random baseline)

3. Standard Few-Shot (5-shot): 47.41% accuracy
   - 65.5% of full performance with 5 examples per class
   - Improvement from 1-shot: +12.86% (37.2% relative gain)

4. Medium Few-Shot (10-shot): 57.39% accuracy
   - 79.3% of full performance
   - Improvement from 5-shot: +9.98%

5. High Few-Shot (20-shot): 59.69% accuracy
   - 82.5% of full performance
   - Improvement from 10-shot: +2.30%
   - Diminishing returns evident (smaller gains as shots increase)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• With just 5 labeled examples per disease class, MVPDR achieves
  65.5% of full-training performance
• This enables rapid deployment for new diseases with minimal labeling effort
• 20-shot achieves 82.5% performance, providing strong results
  with practical data collection requirements
